LumaProfile 0.1.3 is a preview for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 15 or newer.

**Read first**

- This build is ad-hoc signed, **not notarized and not Gatekeeper-approved**. macOS will ask you to approve it once: right-click the app and choose **Open**, or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- It was built and packaged by GitHub Actions from this tag. The packaged DMG has not been installed on real hardware.
- The change in this version (the floating prompt) has **not been checked on a real screen yet**.
- Earlier versions of the source were run on one setup (Mac mini, Dell S3221QS, X-Rite i1 Display Pro). The hardware checklist is incomplete: meter unplug mid-run, force-quit recovery, sleep handling, Restore Before after activation, and other displays or meters are unverified.
- Needs an X-Rite i1Display Pro or Plus colorimeter.

**What's new in 0.1.3**

- When a calibration is waiting for you, a "Waiting for you" panel with Continue and Cancel now floats at the bottom of the screen being measured, above the white measuring window. Before, that window could cover LumaProfile on a small or single screen and hide the prompt.

**From 0.1.2**

- New four-step interface, Adjust Live brightness gauge, Before | After switch, clearer Prepare checklist, dated profile names, and safer cancel and recovery.

Generating a profile does not activate it; activation and verification are a separate, confirmed step. Measurements stay on your Mac. The DMG includes the matching LumaProfile source, the ArgyllCMS 3.5.0 source, and licenses.
